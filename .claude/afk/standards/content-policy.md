# Content Policy Template

The template a full featured site copies to publish its content policy,
per UPLOAD-2 in `site.md`. Copy the block below into the site, replace
every placeholder, and answer every TODO before uploads open to users. A
TODO left in the published policy fails UPLOAD-2.

The template is a starting point, not legal advice. The owner confirms it
against the law where the site operates, and the site's `CLAUDE.md` records
when the policy was last reviewed and by whom.

Placeholders: `<site-name>` the site's name, `<operator>` the business or
person that operates it, `<report-link>` the Report Infringement form or
address from UPLOAD-3, `<policy-date>` the date this version takes effect.

````markdown
# Content Policy

Effective <policy-date>. This policy covers everything you upload to
<site-name>, which <operator> operates. By uploading, you agree to it.

## What you may upload

You may upload only content you created yourself, or content you have
permission to share. When you upload, you confirm that you own the content
or have the rights or permission to use it here.

## What you may not upload

- Content that infringes anyone's copyright, trademark or other rights.
- Content that is illegal, or that promotes illegal activity.
- Sexual content involving minors, in any form. It is reported to the
  authorities.
- Threats, harassment, or content that targets a person or group with hate.
- Another person's private information, such as their address, phone
  number or identity documents, without their consent.
- Malware, or anything meant to harm the site or its users.
- Spam, or content that misleads people about who you are.
````
